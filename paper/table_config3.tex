\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Value function and candidate}}\\
Value function & $b_i=0.1\cdot$median $d_i$, $h=\sum_i\relu(b_i/d_i-1)^2$, $\gamma$ of the step setting, with $q_{90}$ the 90th percentile of $\qex$ over admissible samples\\
Shaping cost & $\kappa=0.3$ if the inner layer holds at least $1\,\%$ of $2\cdot10^5$ box samples, and $\kappa=0$ otherwise. The function $\rho$ is a smooth step from $\gamma\qex=c_I/2$ to $c_I$. The share on the sinusoid is $1.01\,\%$, close to this threshold\\
Network & $\mathcal N_\theta$: 64-64-64 tanh. Features: normalized state (angles as sine and cosine), $\tanh(\zeta/3)$, $\tanh(\tfrac12\ln(d_i/\mathrm{median}\,d_i))$ for the constraints on $\Delta_D$ and $\|\nabla\varpi\|^{-1}$. Softplus sharpness $\beta=10$. The constant $q_0$ is the first percentile of $\qex$ on $2\cdot10^5$ collocation points\\
Initialization & The network starts close to $\Phi_\theta=\psi$, which lies below the exact solution\\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Training}}\\
Collocation & $4\cdot10^5$ Sobol box samples, $10^5$ near the faces, $10^5$ next to the singular set (admissible ones kept)\\
Step setting & Long: $\tau=2$ (80 RK4 substeps), $\gamma=1/q_{90}$, $w_{\mathrm{ex}}=10$. Short: $\tau=0.2$ (8 RK4 substeps), $\gamma=3/q_{90}$, $w_{\mathrm{ex}}=0$. Each system keeps the setting whose seed-0 estimate, after level selection and the final test and before fine-tuning, holds the larger share of box samples. The regression loss and the obstacle penalty of a point whose step leaves $\Oad$ are multiplied by $1+w_{\mathrm{ex}}$\\
Target network & Copy of the averaged weights (decay $0.999$), refreshed every epoch\\
Optimization & Adam, 100 epochs, batch 4096, cosine learning rate $10^{-3}\to10^{-5}$. Exponential loss weight $e^{2\min(V_\theta,V_{\max})}$ with $V_{\max}=3$ (no such weight on the quadrotor)\\
Decrease penalty & Weight 10, on $\relu((L_{f_{\mathrm{cl}}}\Phi_\theta+\kappa\rho)/a)\,e^{-V_\theta}$, where $\Psi_\theta>\psi$\\
Obstacle penalty & Weight 10, on $\relu(\psi+0.01-\Psi_\theta)\,e^{-V_\theta}$, where $\Psi_\theta\le\psi$ and $L_{f_{\mathrm{cl}}}\psi+\kappa\rho>0$\\
Finite-step penalty & Weight 100 (10 on the quadrotor), unit step with 40 RK4 substeps, on $\relu(e^{-(V_\theta(x)-\chi[\gamma\relu(\qex(x)-\qex(x^{+}))+C_1])}-e^{-V_\theta(x^{+})})$ with $\chi=0.2$ and $x^{+}$ the end of the unit step from $x$, on steps in $\Oad$ with $V_\theta(x)<8$\\
Violating states & Every 5 epochs up to epoch 90: the $10^4$ states whose unit step violates the decrease most (no margin), in a pool of $2\cdot10^5$ box samples and $5\cdot10^4$ next to the singular set, 4 Gaussian neighbours (standard deviation $0.01$ of the half-widths), rolling buffer $10^5$\\
